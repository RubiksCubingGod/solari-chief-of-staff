# Deferred

- Per-person reminder hour. The scan holds reminders until 09:00 on the person's clock (`REMINDER_SEND_FROM_HOUR`); a column on `users` would let them choose. Deferred from `daily-scan-reminders`.
- Re-arming a `skipped_unbound` reminder when the person binds a chat inside the same window. Today the skip is final for that day and the next day's reminders go out normally. Deferred from `daily-scan-reminders`.
- Dashboard form fields for `reminderLeadDays`, `autoCancel` and `autoCancelLeadDays`. `POST /calendar-items` and the chat tool `add_calendar_item` take them; the web form still creates entries with the defaults. Deferred from `auto-cancel-enqueue`.
- Editing an entry (`PATCH /calendar-items/:id`) and re-arming a renewal whose cancellation failed. Today a failed cancellation is final for that renewal date (the entry is marked `needs_attention` with the reason) and the next renewal date arms afresh. Deferred from `auto-cancel-enqueue`.
- A bot reply the ledger refuses as `unknown_question` (the task re-asked between the bot's read and its write) is told the question closed, and the task stays parked on the newer question until the next message. Rare, self-healing, and a reply of its own would be nicer. Deferred from `auto-cancel-enqueue`.
- The assistant on `pnpm bot`. The bot process answers an ordinary message with one sentence and carries replies to waiting tasks; the chat loop joins it once the server-to-server credential (`CrudCredential`, `packages/agent/src/crud.ts`) is decided. Deferred from `telegram-roundtrip`.
- `scripts/bot.mjs` with `TELEGRAM_TRANSPORT=webhook`. The runtime serves `webhookHandler()`, nothing mounts it on a listener, and the process refuses rather than registering a URL it will not answer. Deferred from `telegram-roundtrip`.
- The live round-trip on this machine. No `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` is here, so `telegram-roundtrip-live` is a recorded finding until the owner runs `node scripts/live-telegram.mjs` with the phone (README, "The live Telegram round-trip") and pastes the `live_telegram_roundtrip` line into the handoff. Deferred from `telegram-roundtrip`.
